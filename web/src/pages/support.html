Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions about the Holibase app for iPhone and the website. If yours is not here, write to hello@holibase.org. We usually reply within a few days.

Holidays for my region are missing or wrong

In the app, open the menu (…) and choose Report a mistake, or use the form at holibase.org/submit. Add a link to the official calendar of the ministry, canton, council or school district. We check every date against that document; confirmed dates appear in the app and in subscribed calendars, usually within a day.

In some regions, schools or municipalities set their own holidays, for example in parts of Switzerland. There a single calendar for the whole region does not exist; the app says so instead of showing wrong dates.

How do I add the holidays to my calendar?

Tap Subscribe in the app and choose school holidays, public holidays or both. Your calendar app then adds them as a subscription and picks up changes by itself. For other calendar apps, such as Google Calendar or Outlook, tap Copy link for other calendar apps and add the link there as a subscribed calendar.

To remove a subscription on iPhone, open the Calendar app, tap Calendars, then the info button next to the Holibase calendar, and Delete Calendar.

How do I set up the widgets?

Touch and hold the home screen or lock screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and search for Holibase. If you saved more than one region, touch and hold the widget and choose Edit Widget to pick the region it shows.

How does the planner work?

The planner looks at the public holidays of your region and your work days and suggests stretches of nine days or more that need the fewest vacation days. With Match school holidays it only suggests days inside the school breaks. Suggestions are a help for planning; check them against your employer’s rules.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There is no account, no tracking and no advertising. Your saved regions stay on your iPhone. Details are in the privacy policy.
