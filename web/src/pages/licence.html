Every calendar in the API names its source document and the date it was retrieved, so you can check it against the original.

Calendars from OpenHolidays stay under the Open Database License: the API marks them with source_id: "openholidays" and license: "ODbL-1.0" and adds their credit line to the response. Showing them in an app or on a page only needs that credit; if you republish them as a database, it stays under ODbL.

02. Accuracy

Check the dates that matter.

  • Verified calendars come from official open data, a maintained library or a researched calendar checked against the authority’s document.
  • Unverified calendars are returned only on request and are marked as such on the website.
  • Schools can still change their calendar, for example after snow days. For important decisions, confirm with the school or district.
  • Found a mistake? Open an issue on GitHub with a link to the official calendar.

03. Privacy

No account, analytics only with consent.

Holibase needs no account and stores no personal data. Google Analytics only runs if you accept it in the cookie banner, and you can change that any time under Cookie settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
